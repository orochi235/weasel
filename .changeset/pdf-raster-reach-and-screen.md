`@weasel-js/pdf` rasterizes a group with effects over the group's bounds grown by how far its effects reach, rather than over the whole page, so a small blurred badge no longer costs a full-page bitmap. An `Effect` can now say how far it reaches with the new optional `reach`, in device pixels; `blur` sets it, and `effectsReach` (`@weasel-js/renderer`) adds up a stack's. An effect without one, such as `vignette`, still covers the whole page. The renderer's retained surfaces use the same bound, and `commandsBounds` takes the scale the commands will be drawn at.

`screen`-unit paint inside a rasterized group now lands in the PDF where the vector path puts it, in page points rather than raster pixels. `WeaselRenderer.render` takes `screen` in its options, naming what screen space is when it is not the drawing surface, and `renderSceneToPixels` and a raster session's `render` pass it through as `screen`.
