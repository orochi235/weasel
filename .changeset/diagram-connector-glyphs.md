The router commands (Straight, Orthogonal, Curved, Around shapes) and Line jumps carry glyphs, `diagram:routerStraight`, `diagram:routerOrthogonal`, `diagram:routerCurved`, `diagram:routerAvoid` and `diagram:lineJumps`, so an action bar or a menu drawing them shows an icon.
