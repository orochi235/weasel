F2 opens a lens that follows the pointer, and F2 again closes it, in any lab with `labLoupe`.

It is the `loupe.quick` action, bound outside the Loupe keymap, so it takes no Alt+L first; its binding id is `loupe.quick` for rebinding, and `QUICK_KEY` names the default.
