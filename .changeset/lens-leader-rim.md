A lens's leader meets a resized body, and its patch, on their drawn rims: an ellipse or a rect of the body's own width and height. It took every rim to lie half the body's width out, so on a body resized to other proportions it stopped short of the rim, ran inside it, or was not drawn at all while the two were plainly apart.
