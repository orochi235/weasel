`BROWSER_RESERVED_CHORDS` adds Mod+Shift+I, J, C and K: Chrome's devtools, console and inspector on Windows and Linux, and Firefox's Browser and Web Console. Bridge and Bridge anchors move off Mod+Shift+J to Alt+J, which is a breaking change for anyone relying on the old default.
