`Bar` honors `showIf` and `enableIf`. A hidden leaf draws no cell, a hidden section no run and no dividers, and a hidden `page`, `tab`, or `panel` group no button. A disabled leaf's cell is disabled, an action's button and the fused segments of a run of flags included. A disabled group a bar opens from a button still opens, to rows that are disabled, since the popover is where their values are read. A condition reads `values` by dotted path, and a path under an entry reads inside that entry's value.
