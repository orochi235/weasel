`@weasel-js/core` exports the helpers path editing builds on, for a package that edits paths on core's public surface.

`locateChild` and `ChildPosition` (where a node sits under its parent), `anchorDataPatch`, `representationOf`, `locksOf`, `anchorFieldsOf` and `SetAnchorFieldParams`, `inputSession`, `insertStyleOf`, `insertBindingBehaviors`, `nudgeSpec`, `nudgeDelta`, `NUDGE_LABEL` and `NudgeDirection`, and `PaintedPath`. `@weasel-js/core/test-seams` adds `stubDepsForTests`, `gridSnapSourceForTests`, `sceneOfPathsForTests`, `snapSessionDepForTests`, `hitTestAreaForTests` and `useInsertDepSourceForTests`.
