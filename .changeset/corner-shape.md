Corner shape: any corner anchor of a path takes a CSS `corner-shape`, and its radius can be changed later. Additive: `PolygonPath` gains `corners`, an optional `CornerSpec` (`{ shape, radius }`) per flat anchor index, where `shape` is one of the six `corner-shape` keywords (`square`, `squircle`, `round`, `bevel`, `scoop`, `notch`) or a finite `superellipse()` parameter, and `radius` is how far along each edge the cut starts. Following CSS Borders 4, every keyword is a point on one parameter, k: `square` is ∞, `squircle` 2, `round` 1, `bevel` 0, `scoop` -1, `notch` -∞. `cornerShapePieces(k, frame)` draws a corner for any k as the quarter superellipse `|u|^n + |v|^n = 1`, `n = 2^|k|`, spanned from the corner's center for k > 0 and from the outer corner for k < 0, fitted with cubics within `CORNER_FIT_TOLERANCE` of the setback and exact at k = 0, ±1, and ±∞. The command stream keeps the bare anchors, and `resolveCorners` expands the specs into plain geometry, each cut clamped so that the two cuts on a segment never overlap; `resolveCornersWithSources` also answers which source anchor each expanded anchor came from, and `remapPerAnchor` carries a per-anchor array across, so the renderer draws `vertexColors` on a cornered path with every point of a cut in its corner's color. Every reader of a path's geometry calls it first: tessellation, `extractPolylines`, the hit tests, `boundsOfPath`, `pathDistanceToPoint`, the booleans, `offsetPath`, the renderer's path draw, and `serializePathD`, which `@weasel-js/svg` now exports. A spec rides on its anchor through `pathToAnchors` / `anchorsToPath` (`PenAnchor.corner`), so anchor inserts and deletes keep it in place, and `transformPath` scales radii with the geometry. Also new: `cornerFrames` and `cornerFrameAt` (where each corner anchor can be cut, and how far), `withCorner`, `cornerAt`, `cornerSpecAt`, `cornerShapeK`, `cornerKeywordOf`, `isCornerShape`, `nextCornerShape`, and `CORNER_KEYWORDS`.

In path edit, each corner anchor shows a widget just inside it, a small tile cut in the corner's shape. A drag on one (`editCornerAction`, hit as `corner:N`, matched by routing's new `isCornerWidget`) sets the corner's radius live and commits one undo step; pressing the widget of a selected anchor sets every selected corner. `cycleCornerShapeAction`, in the `anchors` group, steps the selected corners through the keywords, and `setCornersAction` sets their shape or radius from its `shape` and `radius` params, typed for `<SceneCanvas tune>` in `ActionParamSchema`. `CORNER_CONTROLS`, `cornerControlsState`, `cornerControlsParams`, and the `useCornerControls` hook are a prefs-form panel over the selected corners: the keywords, a curvature slider from -4 to 4, and the radius, mixed where the corners differ.

A `PathEditHost` can own the anchor selection too, through optional `selectedAnchors` and `setSelectedAnchors`, so an app seeds which anchors start selected and shows what is selected. Giving a sharp corner a shape, through `setCorners` or `cycleCornerShape`, also gives it a radius: the call's own, or a quarter of the room the corner has.
