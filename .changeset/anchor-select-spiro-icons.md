The anchor selection commands (`growAnchorSelection`, `shrinkAnchorSelection`, `selectEveryNthAnchor`, `selectCornerAnchors`, `selectSmoothAnchors`, `invertAnchorSelection`) and each of `setSpiroKind`'s variants have icons, so an action bar shows them as glyphs rather than as text. They are `path-tools:` glyphs under the new "Anchor selection" and "Spiro points" groups.
