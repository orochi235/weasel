The `@experimental` designation is retired. Everything that carried it is now ordinary public API: the actions registry and its built-in action descriptors and icons, the ambient pointer and selection contexts, `CanvasView`, the canvas extension API, the view resolver and viewport layer, the affordance helpers, path-tools' commands and icons, and `registerEffect`. No signatures changed.
