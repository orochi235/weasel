`features={['whiteboard']}` turns a canvas into a whiteboard: the pencil, text and sticky-note tools on a canvas you pan and zoom.

The preset is also exported as `whiteboardPreset`; its tools are text-tools' `TEXT_TOOLS`. It includes the `view`, `pick`, `move`, `resize` and `edit` presets.
