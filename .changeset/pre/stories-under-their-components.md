Stories that were filed as components of their own now sit under the component they show: the arc labs are `On An Arc` stories of `Bar`, `RadioGroup`, `Slider`, `SwatchStrip`, `Tabs` and `ToggleBar`, `Along`'s labs are stories of `Along` beside a new `Default` story, the Prefs and ControlPanel extras are stories of `Prefs` and `ControlPanel`, `Keyboard Layouts` is a story of `KeyDiagram`, and the effect chart lab is `EffectChart`'s `Lab`. Their story ids change with their titles. Lab screens carry the `lab` tag.
