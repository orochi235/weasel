The Nudge Anchors actions draw a glyph of their own: a hatched block pushed by a two-ring pulse behind it, turned the way each one moves. `core:nudgeAnchors` points right and stands for nudging with no direction given; `core:nudgeAnchorsUp`, `core:nudgeAnchorsDown` and `core:nudgeAnchorsLeft` are the other three. Before, those actions had no icon, and a surface showed their arrow keys in its place.
