A `separator` leaf draws a rule and holds no value. `PrefsForm` draws it as a line across the rows, and a bar draws it as an upright rule in a cell of its own, so it stays where it was put even at the bar's end. `PrefSchemaEditor`'s palette has a Separator tool to drag one in, with a new `formSeparator` icon.
