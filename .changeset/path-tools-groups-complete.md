`@weasel-js/path-tools/groups` names the group of every path-tools action, `constraints`, `nudgeAnchors`, and `text` among them.

```tsx
import * as pathToolsGroups from '@weasel-js/path-tools/groups';
<ActionBar group={pathToolsGroups.constraints} />
```
