The `path-tools:roughen` and `path-tools:puckerBloat` glyphs are redrawn so each reads at 16px. Roughen is a square whose edge is finely toothed, where it was a square with its corners knocked out of place. Pucker & Bloat shows both sides of the effect, pinched to spikes on its left half and bloated round on its right, where it was a four-point star.
