'@weasel-js/scales': minor

New package `@weasel-js/scales`: linear, log, power, warped, time, band, ordinal and polar scales, their ticks, and the tick formatter.

It needs only `@weasel-js/geom`, so a slider, a ruler or a server laying out an axis takes it without plot's scene graph.
