The loupe's zoom and mode switch ring it outside its rim, on one arc, and show only while the pointer is near the lens or presses it.

`icons` and `modeIcons` give each mode an icon, drawn in its segment with the caption beside it. A round lens with no `bounds` starts at (48, 48), clear of the canvas edge for its ring.
