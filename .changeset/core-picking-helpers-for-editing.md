`@weasel-js/core` exports the picking and selection helpers editing's tools build on, for a package building tools on its public surface.

The pickers and their parts: `pickWalk`, `adapterPickSource`, `scenePickSource`, `scenePlaneOf`, `PickQuery`, `centersBetween`, `rangeAmong`, `ownClipOf` and `isEnvelope`. For the select tool and group entry: `MULTI_RESIZE_TARGET_ID`, `THROUGH_CHROME`, `createEnterGroupAction`, `createLeaveGroupAction` and `gestureLifecycleParams`; and `snapToContainer`, `kitShapeKindsOf`, `viewerSelection`, and the insert style core owns (`insertStyleOf`, `DEFAULT_STROKE_WIDTH`, `STYLE_OPTION_KEYS`, `InsertStyle`, `StyleOptionKey`). `@weasel-js/core/test-seams` adds `assertFiniteForTests`, `driveForTests`, `PtForTests`, `depsOfForTests`, `makeSceneForTests`, `rectPoseForTests`, `runsForTests`, `tallyForTests`, `timeoutForForTests` and `TestSceneForTests`.
