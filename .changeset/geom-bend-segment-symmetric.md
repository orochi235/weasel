`bendSegmentSymmetric` bends a segment and its images under a set of symmetries.

It bends as `bendSegment` does and writes each handle it moved into that handle's images under the given `AnchorSymmetry`s, beside `translateAnchorsSymmetric` and `moveHandleSymmetric`.
