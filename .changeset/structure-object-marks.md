In the asset editor's structure tree, an `object` leaf whose fields it draws, and a list or map of such objects, now carries a braces glyph. Its fields step in under it and its guide bracket hangs from the glyph; before, the bracket hung from nothing and the fields sat level with the object's own name. In the shelf's Settings tree, a setting now starts its name under its group's name, as in the structure tree.
