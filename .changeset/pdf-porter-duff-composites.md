`@weasel-js/pdf` draws a group's Porter-Duff `composite` exactly instead of source-over. With a `rasterize` hook, a group whose composite is anything but `source-over` is handed to the hook together with everything the page drew before it, inside the groups it sits in, so the renderer composites it onto its real backdrop; the raster is placed on white paper over the group's bounds, or over the whole page for an operator that clears what the group leaves empty. An isolated group holding such a composite is rasterized whole. The hook's signature is unchanged; without one, a composite is still drawn source-over and reported in `skipped`.

`@weasel-js/renderer` exports `leavesUncovered(composite)`, whether an operator leaves the destination as it was wherever the source is empty, and `readsBackdrop(commands)`, whether any group in a list has its own composite.
