The shape tools draw with their tool options: rect, ellipse, polygon, star, line, pencil, and pen declare `fill`, `stroke`, `strokeWidth`, and (for paths) `fillRule`, each shared with every shape tool, and text a `fill` shared under `textFill`. Polygon's sides, star's points and inner radius, the pen's auto-commit-on-close, and the sticky note's paper and ink are per-tool options, and the hooks' `sides`, `points`, `innerRatio`, `autoCommitOnClose`, and the sticky tool's `background` and `fill` set their defaults. Every insert tool takes a `share`. The insert action hands the style to the `insert` dep as `extras.style`; an insert carrying none still cycles the kit palette. The options tune their actions only while their tool is active, beneath any other tuning, and a canvas whose host publishes no `toolOptionValues` keeps them in memory. `useToolOptionParams` reads a tool's options for an options row, re-rendering on a shared change. The tools' option sets are exported (`RECT_TOOL_OPTIONS` and the rest, `shapeToolOptions`, `DEFAULT_SHAPE_PAINT`). Breaking: `<SceneCanvas insertPaint>`, `InsertPaint`, and `paletteInsertPaint` are gone, replaced by the shape tools' options, and `usePenTool.prefs` is gone, replaced by `PEN_TOOL_OPTIONS`.
