The weasel theme has a secondary accent: a honey amber opposite the violet accent, as a `secondary-soft`/`-base`/`-strong` ramp, a `--wzl-secondary` fill, and `--wzl-secondary-fg` for text. `--wzl-secondary-fg` is picked from the ramp to clear 4.5:1 on every surface, so it is the base step in dark mode and the deep step in light. An auto row's `AUTO` readout draws in it.
