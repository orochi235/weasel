A toned property panel, callout, or dialog now sinks its controls' wells one lightness step below its own fill instead of to the theme's near-black sunken surface. Unselected flat `ToggleBar` cells, off `Switch` tracks, slider rails, checkboxes, and fields in a tinted panel stop being the highest-contrast things on it.
