# prefs: saturation

Repeated blind reviews of the area `prefs` (37 files, 3095 lines) at the frozen commit
`ca78c1c42a`, with no fixes between them, from `codex`.
Each occasion is one review, or one ingested report; its findings are matched against the ledger of distinct
defects so far. Reviews stop once fewer than 1 defect per thousand lines is estimated unseen, the chance that the next occasion
finds something new falls under 15%, the next defect would cost over $1.49, or 40 occasions have run.
Not stopped yet. `skulkr scour report prefs` rewrites this file from the ledger.

## Yield by occasion

The estimates on each row are Chao2 over every occasion up to it. "Unseen" is the estimated number of defects no
occasion has found; "next-new" is the chance the next occasion's finding is one of them. "$/defect" is what every
occasion up to the row has cost, divided by the distinct defects found so far.

## Estimates

Chao2 over one source is a floor for what that reviewer can find: its reviews resemble each other, so it misses
the same things every time. Chapman between two sources is better while they find different kinds of defect.
